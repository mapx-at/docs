Examples

Each example runs live on the page, shows the complete code and a single HTML file to copy. The basis is MapLibre GL JS (4.7.1) with pmtiles (3.2.1), no key and no build. Filter by product:

  • Embed a mapA map with the ready-made mapx style in an HTML page.Maps
  • Switch the basemapSwitch between map, aerial imagery and terrain.Maps
  • Time of day and night modeThe default style in four lighting moods.Maps
  • 3D terrainAerial imagery over the terrain model, tilted and exaggerated.Maps · Elevation
  • Elevation on clickA click queries the elevation at a point, terrain or surface.Elevation
  • Elevation profileClick a line, see the profile.Elevation
  • SearchSearch for places, addresses and peaks and fly to them.Search
  • RouteClick start and destination, get the way with length and duration.Navigation
  • Weather overlayTemperature, precipitation or wind as an overlay, hour chosen with a slider.Weather
  • Reachability (isochrones)Which area can be reached in 5, 10 and 15 minutes?Navigation
  • Travel-time matrixTravel times between several places in one request.Navigation
  • Cadastre: parcel on clickParcel boundaries over aerial imagery, a click shows the number.Parcels
  • TileJSON sourcesAdd orthophoto and contours without a ready-made style.Maps
  • 3D buildingsExtruded buildings with BEV heights, camera tilted.Maps
  • Search Box with suggestionsSuggestions while typing, position after the click, type filter.Search
  • Address autofill in a formType a street, pick a suggestion, the form fills itself.Search
  • Address validationCheck an address against the official register and show the corrected result.Search
  • Reverse geocodingClick the map, get the nearest address.Search
  • Truck route with optionsVehicle size, avoid tolls and motorways, alternatives.Navigation
  • Tour planningClick stops, get the best order from the depot and back.Navigation
  • Public transit routeClick start and destination, get walking and riding legs.Transit
  • Departures at a stopClick near a stop, see the next departures.Transit · SDK
  • Traffic flow and incidentsTraffic style plus the active incidents of the visible area.Traffic
  • Static image for a listingClick a place, get a ready-made image URL with a pin.Static images
  • Your own GeoJSON on the mapPoints, line and area over the mapx style, popup on click.Maps
  • SDK: route planner and layersRouteControl with truck profile, LayerControl, search and style switcher.Navigation · SDK

Notes

Section titled “Notes”
  • All data comes from official sources, no OSM data. Attribution: License.
  • All examples call the endpoints under https://api.mapx.at/v1/, without an access token.
  • Examples marked SDK use mapx-gl.
